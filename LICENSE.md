## Permitted Use

This software and all associated files ("the Kit") are provided exclusively for
use by **authorised CSA / CBAC personnel** in the delivery of structured,
declared security awareness training programmes.

Permitted activities include:

- Deploying the Kit in controlled training environments where all participants
  have been informed that a phishing simulation will take place.
- Modifying the Kit for internal training purposes.
- Sharing the Kit with other authorised CSA colleagues via internal channels.

---

## Prohibited Use

The following are strictly prohibited:

- Deploying `phish.html` or any component of the Kit against individuals who
  have **not** given informed consent to participate in a phishing simulation.
- Distributing the Kit outside CSA / CBAC without written authorisation
  from the CSA Director-General.
- Using the Kit for any purpose other than declared security awareness training.
- Retaining `session.log` or any personal data collected during training beyond
  the immediate training session without a lawful basis under the
  **Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843)**.

---

## Data Protection

Personal data collected during training sessions (names, emails, device
information, geolocation, facial images) must be:

- Used solely to demonstrate phishing techniques to participants.
- Deleted at the conclusion of the training session.
- Never shared with third parties.

Processing is conducted under the authority of the
**Cybersecurity Act, 2020 (Act 1038)** for the purpose of public cybersecurity
education, subject to the protections of the **Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843)**.
